        --sizeHow big are your clients?
        
5 to 100 people without a full-time IT person. Or one IT person who needs backup.

      

      

        --hoursWhen can we reach you?
        
Business hours by default. 24/7 if you need it.

      

      

        --on-siteDo you come on site?
        
Yes, across Greater Victoria. Everything else remotely.

      

      

        --hardwareDo we need new laptops?
        
Probably not. We enrol what you have, or lease you new ones with little upfront cost.

      

      

        --accessWho controls our systems?
        
You do. We use named admin accounts with MFA, every change is logged, and you can remove us any time.
