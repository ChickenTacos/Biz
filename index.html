FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

What does a managed IT company actually do?

We act as your IT department. We set up and look after your computers, email, Wi-Fi, phones and backups, keep everything secure, and we're who you call when something goes wrong.

Do we need our own IT person?

No. Most businesses we work with don't have one. If you do, we can support them too.

What are your support hours?

Business hours by default, with 24/7 support available if your business needs it.

Will we need new computers?

Usually not. We'll tell you what's worth keeping, and if you need new equipment, we can lease it to you with little upfront cost.

How big does our business need to be?

We work with businesses of all sizes, from a few people to a hundred or more.

How long does it take to get started?

The free IT check takes about an hour. After that, most businesses are fully set up within a few weeks.
