LANYARD(1)Lanyard ManualLANYARD(1)

      

        
NAME

        
lanyard · managed IT for companies without an IT department

      

      

        
SYNOPSIS

        
lanyard [--fully-managed | --co-managed | --project] [--on-site] your-company

      

      

        
QUESTIONS

        

          -s, --sizeWhat size companies do you work with?
          
Mostly teams of 5 to 100 people with no full-time IT staff. If you have one IT person who needs backup, we do that too.

        

        

          -o, --on-siteDo you come on site?
          
Yes, across Greater Victoria. Everything else we handle remotely, and most day-to-day problems get fixed faster that way.

        

        

          -p, --platformWindows or Mac?
          
Both, plus iPhone, iPad and Android. Most offices are a mix, and that's fine.

        

        

          -h, --hardwareDo we need new laptops?
          
Probably not. We enrol what you already have, then plan replacements on a schedule so nothing dies at a bad moment.

        

        

          -a, --accessWho can get into our systems?
          
Your data stays in your own Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace. We use named admin accounts with multi-factor sign-in, and every change is logged. You can see or remove our access at any time.

        

        

          -n, --nowCan you cut off someone's access right now?
          
Yes. Once you tell us, we block sign-in and end every active session, then tidy up mail, files and devices afterwards.

        

        

          -c, --costWhat does it cost?
          
Managed plans are a monthly fee per person or per device. Projects get a fixed quote after a free intro call. You'll have a number before you commit to anything.
